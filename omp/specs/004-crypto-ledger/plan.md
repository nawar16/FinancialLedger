## Summary

The crypto‑ledger feature implements a tamper‑evident, append‑only ledger for financial documents using Symfony, Doctrine ORM, and cryptographic hashing. The implementation consists of two core services:

- **LedgerHasher** – computes SHA‑256 hashes for each entry and verifies chain integrity.
- **CanonicalSerializer** – normalizes and serializes ledger entries deterministically.

Integration tests (`FinancialLogSecurityTest.php`) confirm that the ledger can be accessed by the `symfony_app` role and that unauthorized modifications are blocked.
